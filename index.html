About

I started a contracting business in 2020 and built it to $100K+ in revenue through door-to-door outreach, Nextdoor and referrals. I tracked every job in spreadsheets, and the numbers showed land clearing paid the most per hour. So I moved my marketing and crew time toward it. In Summer 2026 that meant about $40K in revenue in three months with a team of 3.

That habit of using data to decide where effort goes carries into everything I build. I'm not a software engineer. I work in R, SQL and Excel, and I use AI agents like Claude every day to build and ship real tools: web apps, automations, analyses and multi-agent pipelines with a human approving the important steps.

What I'm best at is the customer side: pitching, demos, scoping and pricing work, and getting a decision-maker to yes. I'm looking for a role where I can put AI to work for customers.
